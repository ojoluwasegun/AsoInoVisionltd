AI and technology in Audit

November 2026 | ASO INOVISION in Nigeria

Share inf

Audit is changing. Records grow every year, risks move faster than an annual sample can follow, and boards, regulators and investors expect evidence they can trace. At ASO INOVISION we help audit, risk and finance teams use AI-driven technology to make audits more risk-based, more data-driven and easier to repeat.

Our work starts with the data an organisation already holds: ledgers, payments, inventory records, site logs, access logs and documents. Instead of testing a sample and hoping it is representative, our models can examine whole populations of transactions and flag the items that do not fit the pattern, so audit time goes to the places where risk is highest.

How we apply AI in audit

  • Risk-based planning. Models rank transactions, branches, sites and processes by risk, so audit effort is aimed where it matters.
  • Whole-population testing. Anomaly detection across complete data sets, with every flagged item traceable to its source record.
  • Document intelligence. AI assistants read contracts, invoices and reports, extract key terms and match them to the records, with a person reviewing every decision.
  • Continuous monitoring. Dashboards watch key controls between audits, so exceptions surface when they happen, not months later.
  • Physical evidence. Access control and smart lock logs, and camera analytics where appropriate, link what happened on site to what the records say.

Human judgment stays in charge

AI finds patterns. It does not sign an opinion. In every engagement an experienced person reviews each flagged item, and we document how each model was built, what data it used and how it performed, so the work can be explained to management, regulators and investors. ASO INOVISION provides technology and advisory support. Statutory audit opinions are issued only by licensed audit firms.

Built for Nigerian conditions

Data in Nigerian organisations often sits in spreadsheets, scanned documents and mixed systems, and power and connectivity are not always dependable. We design for that reality, with data preparation, tools that tolerate poor connections and clear handover documentation, rather than assuming a perfect data warehouse.

How an engagement runs

We follow the same four moves every time: diagnose the workflow and the data, prototype against real records, deploy with monitoring, and scale with training and handover. Your team keeps the tools and the knowledge when we leave.

If your organisation wants audits that are faster to run and easier to defend, we would be glad to talk.
