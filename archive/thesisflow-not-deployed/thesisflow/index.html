Good to know

What does Research Collaboration mean?

You are not handed a template. We work through the project with you, from topic to analysis, so the work reflects your study and is ready for your supervisor.

Do you write my thesis for me?

No. You stay the author of your work. We coach, review and explain, so you understand and can defend every part of it.

Which package should I pick?

Pick Foundation if you want guidance by message. Pick Refined if you want review rounds after supervisor feedback. Pick Distinction if you want calls, voice notes and full defense preparation.

Is printing included?

Printing is not included in any package.

How do I get started?

Tap any WhatsApp button, tell us your topic, department and deadline, and we will confirm the best package and payment plan for you.
